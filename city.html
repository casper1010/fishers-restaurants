The New Town

Award-winning Fishers in the City is tucked into a converted warehouse on Thistle Street, right in the middle of Edinburgh's city centre.

A fixture since 2001, it's warm and unhurried, made for long lunches or celebratory dinners. Order a bowl of mussels or go all in on the hot shellfish platter—either way, it's the same refined seafood cooking that made the original Fishers a local favourite.

Don't fancy fish? No problem—there's just as much on offer from the land.

It's a celebration of all things coastal, claws and all, and each dish feels like it's been refined over years of trading.
