The Original

A landmark on Leith's historic Shore, Fishers has been one of the city's favourite spots for seafood for over three decades.

Set in a seventeenth-century stone watchtower, it has two dining areas to choose from: the cosy round room near the bar, or the main restaurant — not forgetting a table outside by the water when the sun's out. Come see us for proper fish, fresh salads, and fine steaks in a setting that never gets old.

Seafood cooking with flair and commitment from a small kitchen in boatlike surroundings — here, traditional Scots dishes get an imaginative twist.
