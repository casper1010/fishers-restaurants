A bit of history

Since 1991, we've been serving Edinburgh from a seventeenth-century watchtower on Leith's historic shore. It's a family business — started by mum and dad, now run alongside their two sons — built around fresh, local seafood landed close to home and cooked simply. But there's something for everyone at the table: our meat and vegetarian dishes get just as much care as anything from the sea.

One restaurant has grown into three, but the philosophy hasn't changed since day one — good food, properly prepared, and served like family.

— The Millars
